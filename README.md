For this task I chose to implement an async training version of DQN.
Episode length/return/return avg are all climbing very steeply, suggesting that the algorithm is indeed learning to play these games more optimally over time. There are a few very steep strange valleys in all the graphs, however. All of them rebound back to the original levels/beyond eventually however. I am uncertain why they appear and what they represent. My assumpting is that it is just a an artifact caused by the paralel nature of this algorithm. Perhaps these valleys are formed when less trained agents begin influencing the NN strongly in their undeveloped enviornment observations and they rebound back when the enviornment develops further. Overall this algorithm is clearly functional, however, and provides a good representation of asynch training.
